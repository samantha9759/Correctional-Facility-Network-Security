# Correctional Facility Cybersecurity Threat Assessment

## Purpose

This assessment identifies hypothetical cybersecurity threats that could affect a correctional facility's information systems, personnel, and operational continuity.

## Threat 1: Ransomware Attack

**Description:** Malicious software encrypts or disrupts access to facility information systems.

**Potential Impact:**
- Loss of access to administrative records.
- Disruption of business and support applications.
- Increased recovery time and operating costs.

**Risk Level:** High

**Recommended Controls:**
- Endpoint detection and response.
- Email and phishing protection.
- Network segmentation.
- Protected offline or immutable backups.
- Incident response and recovery procedures.

## Threat 2: Unauthorized Account Access

**Description:** An attacker obtains or misuses an employee account to access information systems.

**Potential Impact:**
- Exposure of confidential information.
- Unauthorized changes to system data.
- Access to resources beyond the employee's job responsibilities.

**Risk Level:** High

**Recommended Controls:**
- Multi-Factor Authentication.
- Role-Based Access Control.
- Account lockout and monitoring policies.
- Regular access reviews.
- Prompt removal of access when employment ends.

## Threat 3: Network Service Disruption

**Description:** A hardware failure, configuration error, or cyberattack interrupts network services.

**Potential Impact:**
- Loss of access to business applications.
- Delays in administrative work.
- Increased dependence on approved continuity procedures.

**Risk Level:** High

**Recommended Controls:**
- Network equipment monitoring.
- Redundant critical infrastructure where appropriate.
- Configuration backups.
- Documented recovery procedures.
- Periodic continuity exercises.

## Threat Prioritization

| Threat | Likelihood | Impact | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ransomware | Medium | High | High |
| Unauthorized Account Access | Medium | High | High |
| Network Service Disruption | Medium | High | High |

These ratings are illustrative assumptions for this fictional project, not results from a real facility assessment.

## Conclusion

Correctional facilities require dependable technology and controlled access to information systems. Cybersecurity planning should address both information protection and operational continuity.

A combination of preventive controls, monitoring, backups, and tested response procedures can reduce the impact of security incidents.

## Scope Disclaimer

This assessment is hypothetical and intended for educational and professional portfolio purposes. It does not describe actual facility vulnerabilities or operational procedures.
